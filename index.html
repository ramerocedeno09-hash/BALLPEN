Our Community Purpose

Saved locally in your browser

This AI image classification tool empowers students, educators, and local residents with instant, low-cost visual recognition. By running lightweight machine learning directly in any web browser without expensive hardware or internet cloud fees, our community can rapidly sort, identify, and analyze visual objects—supporting sustainability, education, and smarter local decision-making.

STEM & Digital Literacy

Demystifies artificial intelligence for youth and students by letting them see neural network confidence scores react in real time.

Zero-Cost Accessibility

Hosted freely on GitHub Pages and powered by browser compute—removing financial barriers for schools, barangays, and nonprofits.

Privacy-First Design

Camera frames and uploaded photos never leave the user's device, safeguarding citizen privacy while delivering instant insights.

Rapid Local Action

Accelerates sorting, quality checks, and visual identification workflows so volunteers and workers can focus on high-impact tasks.

UI/UX Engineering

Built on All Principles of Design

Every layout decision on this webpage intentionally applies foundational visual design principles for clarity, harmony, and usability.

1. Balance

Asymmetric 7:5 hero split balanced by symmetrical 4-column feature grids and a 50/50 interactive AI workspace.

2. Contrast

Deep slate-950 canvas paired with crisp white typography, luminous indigo/emerald accents, and high-contrast confidence bars.

3. Emphasis

The live AI Scanner and Top Identified Class card command focal attention via glowing borders, scale, and animated scanlines.

4. Hierarchy

Distinct typographic scale from 6xl display headings down to section titles, card headers, and uppercase micro-labels.

5. Alignment

Anchored to a strict 12-column responsive container with consistent left-aligned reading axes and baseline-aligned controls.

6. Repetition

Unified rounded-2xl/3xl card geometry, consistent icon containers, pill badges, and cohesive border treatments throughout.

7. Proximity

Related actions—like mode tabs, camera buttons, and live probability bars—are tightly grouped into intuitive functional zones.

8. White Space

Generous vertical section padding and open internal card breathing room prevent visual clutter and improve cognitive focus.
